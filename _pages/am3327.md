# [Anshuman Mohan](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~amohan/)

### [Picnic czar](../projects/picnic)

Chicken served at CS Picnic Fall '25

Get a whole big thing of full-fat plain Greek yogurt. Add two tablespoons of salt, and a tablespoon each of cumin, ground coriander, dried mint, oregano, dried dill, cayenne, and paprika. Taste. It should taste way too salty, but try to sorta look through that and see if you want more heat (via cayenne), more earthiness (via cumin+coriander), etc. Add 8lbs of boneless skinless chicken thighs, each of which you have cut in half. Massage. Refrigerate overnight, and up to a day. Roast at 375 for an hour. At picnic location, grill for color and smokiness.
